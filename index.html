OUR FOUNDER

Meet Professor Joseph Mba

Genial Laurel Schools was founded with a vision to provide an education that combines academic excellence, character development, innovation and global relevance.

Professor Joseph Mba is an accomplished educationist whose teaching and research experience spans Nigeria and Canada. His passion is to raise a generation of learners who are confident, disciplined, creative and prepared to thrive anywhere in the world.

Under his leadership, Genial Laurel Schools integrates the Nigerian and Canadian Curriculum to equip every learner with knowledge, values and practical skills for lifelong success.
